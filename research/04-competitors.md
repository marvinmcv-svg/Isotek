## C. Award-level motion/interaction references [prior knowledge — not re-verified this session]

| Reference | Idea to adapt |
|---|---|
| Awwwards SOTD-style editorial sites with oversized condensed type (e.g. agency portfolios using variable-width fonts) | Animate the **width axis** of the display font on scroll/hover — "tension" in the type |

## D. Market clichés and openings

**Clichés in industrial/EPC sites in the region:** night refinery stock hero; blue-gradient overlays; hard-hat stock models; logo carousels as the main proof; three identical service cards; "soluciones integrales" with no specifics; sliders.

**Openings Isotek can claim:**
1. **Engineering-as-aesthetic** — the single-line diagram as the visual language. Nobody local does it.
2. **Named proof** — 16 named case studies with blue-chip clients (YPFB, Cargill, AB InBev/Cervepar, CBN, Alicorp, Soboce). Lead with them.
3. **"Fabricado en Bolivia"** — own CNC lines, own PROTOS brand, IEC-certified. A pride and lead-time story competitors can't tell.
4. **Only E+H-certified calibration lab in Bolivia** (brand claim) — a unique, concrete service hook.
5. **One supplier, one line** — the EPC promise expressed literally by the busbar motif.
